One URL does the whole job.

A crabcrop URL reads left to right: a signature, then the operations, then the source. Each of your five choices is one part of it, and lights up in the bar above.

Signed URLs, from any stack

Every URL carries an HMAC-SHA256 signature over the operations and the source. Same inputs, same URL, from any of these:

  • TypeScript
  • Python
  • Rust
  • curl
