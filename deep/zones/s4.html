Provenance, checked and signed

Pictures can carry Content Credentials, a signed record of where they came from. crabcrop reports the record a source carries, and with your own certificate it signs every still JPEG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF it serves, with the source as its parent.

Private sources blocked by default

The loader refuses sources on loopback, private, and other non-public addresses, checked again on every redirect. Allowlisting a host is how you vouch for an internal one.
