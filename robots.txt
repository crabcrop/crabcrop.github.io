# crabcrop.org is three pages, / (index.html), /docs.html and
# /under-the-sea/ (the dive with Snap), plus the files they load, and two
# previews, /preview/dive/ and /preview/dive-gpu/ (which now sends
# visitors on to /under-the-sea/), each carrying its own noindex.
# Crawlers may read all of it.
#
# The old /preview/ design experiments were deleted, and so was
# /preview/home/ when that page became the home page. They are not listed
# under Disallow on purpose: a deleted page answers 404, and the 404 is
# what drops it from search indexes. A Disallow would hide that 404 from
# crawlers and keep stale entries listed. The previews are not
# disallowed for the same reason: a crawler has to read a page to see its
# noindex.
#
# Do not disallow the images either: link-preview bots such as Twitterbot
# read robots.txt before they fetch the og:image.
