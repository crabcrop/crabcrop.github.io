# crabcrop.org is two pages, / (index.html) and /docs.html, plus the
# files they load, and one preview, /preview/dive/, which carries its
# own noindex. Crawlers may read all of it.
#
# The old /preview/ design experiments were deleted. They are not listed
# under Disallow on purpose: a deleted page answers 404, and the 404 is
# what drops it from search indexes. A Disallow would hide that 404 from
# crawlers and keep stale entries listed. The dive is not disallowed for
# the same reason: a crawler has to read the page to see its noindex.
#
# Do not disallow the images either: link-preview bots such as Twitterbot
# read robots.txt before they fetch the og:image.
